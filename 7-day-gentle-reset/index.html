Frequently Asked Questions

How much time does it take each day?

The rhythm is designed for about five minutes in the morning and five minutes in the evening. You may spend longer when you want to, but you do not need a large block of uninterrupted time.

Do I have to complete seven days in a row?

No. The seven-day structure gives you a clear path, but you are welcome to move at the pace that supports you best. Missing a day is not failure; simply return when you are ready.

Should I start with the free Starter Kit?

The free Starter Kit is a helpful first step if you want to experience the approach before beginning the full reset. You may also begin directly with the 7-Day Gentle Reset.
