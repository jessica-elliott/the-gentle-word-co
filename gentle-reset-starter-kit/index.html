Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Starter Kit really free?

Yes. The Gentle Reset Starter Kit is completely free. It was created to give you a meaningful place to begin before deciding whether the full 7-Day Gentle Reset is right for you.

Do I need to print it?

It was designed to be printed on standard US Letter paper, but you may also use it digitally with a PDF annotation app. Choose whichever experience feels easiest and most supportive for you.

How much time will I need?

There is no required pace. You can move through the Starter Kit in one quiet sitting or take your time with each section. The goal is not to finish quickly or perfectly—it is simply to begin.
